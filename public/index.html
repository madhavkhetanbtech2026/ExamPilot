Autonomy ratio heuristic

How is this calculated, and what does it NOT show?

Active-thinking points = guided questions × 1.5 + quiz answers × 1.2 (scaled by the AI-rated quality of your written reasoning) + exam answers marked × 2 + peer rooms × 2.5. Shortcut penalty = direct answers × 2. Autonomy ratio = points ÷ (points + penalty), kept between 10% and 100%. Leaderboard score = points × ratio ÷ 10.

The weights are our own design choices, not scientific constants, and reasoning ratings come from an AI model that can be wrong. This number describes how you use the tool. It does not prove how much you learned.

Reasoning evidence

Each quiz answer needs written reasoning, and the AI rates it from 1 to 5. If the tool builds thinking, these ratings should rise with practice.
